Seal 7

Introduction

If you have not already done so, please read the Rapture study first. The events from Seal 7 follows it directly and builds on the idea that the Rapture does not happen “In a moment, in the twinkling of an eye”.

New Jerusalem

First, let’s start by identifying New Jerusalem in Revelation. For this we have to go back to the Revelation 12 sign.


In Revelation 17, it is talking about the great whore, but this shows us that a woman in prophesy is a city.

Rapture

Now if we jump to the Rapture, we see that this same woman / city of Revelation 12 being referred to as “she” bringing a Man Child who will rule all nations.


The first word for “child” is and is also translated as Son of Man in other places in the Bible.

And the second word for “child” is and is often translated as “children” in other paces is the Bible.

To put this into my own words then, Revelation 12 is saying: “The city brought forth the Son of Man, who was to rule all nations with a rod of iron: and her children was caught up unto God, and to his throne.”

This fits the Rapture perfectly, but what is does not show us is what happens immediately after. Most churches teaches that we go to heaven, but I have not found anything in Revelation that shows we go to heaven after the Rapture. Instead, what we see in the next verse is the city fleeing into the wilderness, and nowhere in the Bible is Heaven described as a wilderness.


The 1260 days is what is used to link Revelation 12:6 to Trumpet 1 in the Revelation Timeline. This happens about 7 months or 210 days after the Rapture.

So what happens during those 7 months? This is where we get into Seal 7.

Seal 7

Thing about what is going to happen at the end of the 1000 years of peace or even right after the rapture. Let’s say two people are married, if there is no marriage beyond either of these points, there is only two outcomes that I can think of:

  1. Everyone that is married then just stops loving each other. Sort of like saying: thanks, it was fun, but I don't want you anymore.
  2. People will still love each other and then Yah says, sorry, you are not allowed to love each other anymore and then separates them.

The "Contradiction"?

So then how do we address this seeming contradiction of marriage forever and no marriage after the resurrection?


For most of my life I just assume that the resurrection was referring to the rapture. But if we look in Revelation we can see that there are 3 resurrections:

  1. During the rapture.
  2. When the 2 Witnesses are raised from the dead.
  3. At the end of the 1000 years of peace, when everyone is resurrected (Judgement Day).

Now look at the wording in Luke above were Yahshua said “accounted worthy to obtain that world”, which world is He talking about. I believe the “accounted worthy” can only refer to the new world, when everything will be made new. So after judgement day:


We see that there will be no more death. We also see that divorce was not intended from the beginning, which would fit the no more sorrow part. In a perfect world, can divorce still exist?


If there is no death and no divorce, does it not mean that married people will be together forever?
